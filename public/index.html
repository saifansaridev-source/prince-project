MEDIATOR BUSINESS MODEL

Fair & Transparent: The 2% Brokerage Model

Ghar Bazaar acts as the reliable mediator between renters and property owners/brokers. We eliminate inflated arbitrary commissions by establishing a clear 2% brokerage calculation on successfully finalized rental deals.

• No advance or hidden registration fees for renters.
• Deals are only marked completed when the tenant and broker agree.
• Complete digital recording for institutional accountability.

Brokerage Calculation Example

Agreed Monthly Rental Deal ₹20,000
Platform Brokerage Rate 2.0%
Recorded Brokerage Fee ₹400
Formula: Agreed Deal Amount × 0.02 = Platform Brokerage Recorded
